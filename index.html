Quiet forms.
Lasting light.

Hand-finished jewelry designed in small numbers — each piece a study in proportion, material, and the spaces between.

01 — Necklace

The Continuous Line

A single unbroken chain of 18k gold, refined to near-weightlessness. Designed to rest against the collarbone with almost no presence until light finds it.

02 — Ring

Solstice Band

A narrow circle of brushed gold, its surface interrupted only by a single polished edge. Made to mark time without announcing it.

03 — Earrings

Suspended Form

Two elongated drops of solid gold, hollowed from within so they catch air and movement. Lightweight enough to be worn from morning until late.

04 — Bracelet

Open Circle

An open cuff in warm yellow gold, its ends gently tapered. The gap is intentional — a small interval that keeps the form from closing in on itself.

05 — Pendant

Single Point

A small disc of high-polish gold suspended on a fine chain. Nothing more. Designed as a quiet anchor for everyday wear.

About

Between London and Lisbon.

Liora Atelier is the independent practice of a designer working slowly between two cities. Each piece begins as a question of proportion — how gold sits against skin, how light finds an edge, how little is needed for something to feel complete.

Work is refined through repeated making. Forms are reduced until only the essential remains. Pieces are produced in limited numbers, finished by hand, and meant to be worn every day without ceremony.

The studio does not follow seasons. Collections grow quietly over time, guided by material honesty and the quiet presence of gold.

— Liora
